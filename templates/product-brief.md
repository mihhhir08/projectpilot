# Product Brief

## Product Goal

Describe the outcome in one sentence, without prescribing the implementation.

## Target User

Identify the primary user, their context, and the job they need to complete.

## Problem

Explain the current friction and why solving it matters now.

## Core Workflows

List the two to five user journeys that define the product's value.

## Must-Haves

Define the smallest complete release.

## Non-Goals

Record what this version intentionally excludes.

## Success Criteria

Use observable product or user outcomes rather than implementation activity.

## Assumptions

List decisions that were inferred and still need validation.

## Open Questions

Include only questions that could materially change scope or architecture.
